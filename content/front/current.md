My [lab](https://www.dienerlab.com/) at the [Medical University of Graz](https://medunigraz.at) is located
within the Diagnostic and Research Institute of Hygiene, Microbiology and Environmental Medicine.
We also are a part of the Austrian FWF Cluster of Excellence [*Microbiomes Drive Planetary Health*](https://microplanet.at/).

We study the human gut microbiome by combining ecology, systems biology and metabolism. In particular,
we investigate how microbes interact with each other, their host and their environment, and how those
interactions affect health and disease. Our research includes microbial nutrient use and metabolism,
the effects of microbes on dietary compounds and drugs, and the design of microbiome interventions.
We combine computational approaches such as metagenomics, metabolomics and modeling with wet lab
experiments, including single-cell culturomics. You can learn more about our
[research](https://www.dienerlab.com/research/) and our work on accessible science, software and teaching
on the [lab website](https://www.dienerlab.com/).

Before that I studied signaling in microbial cultures and metabolic alterations in cancer (you can find more about that
in my [publications](/pubs)).
